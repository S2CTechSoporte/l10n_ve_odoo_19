# Required Tests

This document is the blocking acceptance contract for
`l10n_ve_dual_currency` on Odoo 19.0.

## Accounting Assumptions

- Localization: Venezuela (`l10n_ve`)
- Company country: Venezuela
- Company currency: VES, Venezuelan bolivar soberano
- Reference currency: USD
- Invoice date, 2026-01-10: 1 USD = 50 VES
- Second payment date, 2026-01-20: 1 USD = 52 VES
- Third payment date, 2026-01-30: 1 USD = 54 VES
- Tests do not use taxes unless the behavior under test requires them.

The rates are deterministic test fixtures, not historical or forecast exchange
rates.

## Mandatory Invoice And Payment Matrix

1. USD invoice with one full USD payment.
2. USD invoice with one full VES payment.
3. VES invoice with one full USD payment.
4. VES invoice with one full VES payment.
5. USD invoice with multiple partial USD payments.
6. USD invoice with multiple partial VES payments.
7. USD invoice with mixed USD/VES partial payments on different dates.
8. VES invoice with multiple partial USD payments.
9. VES invoice with multiple partial VES payments.
10. VES invoice with mixed USD/VES partial payments on different dates.

| # | Invoice | Payments | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | USD 100 at 50 | USD 100 at 50 | Both residuals are zero; one USD payment is shown; invoice is settled. |
| 2 | USD 100 at 50 | VES 5,000 at 50 | Native and dual totals remain coherent; both residuals are zero. |
| 3 | VES 5,000 at 50 | USD 100 at 50 | The reference widget shows USD 100; both residuals are zero. |
| 4 | VES 5,000 at 50 | VES 5,000 at 50 | Native VES accounting is unchanged; both residuals are zero. |
| 5 | USD 100 at 50 | USD 40, then USD 60 | USD residual changes from 100 to 60 to 0; two payments are shown. |
| 6 | USD 100 at 50 | VES 2,000, then VES 3,000 | Residual changes consistently in VES and USD and finishes at zero. |
| 7 | USD 100 at 50 | USD 30 at 50, VES 2,080 at 52, USD 30 at 54 | Each payment uses its own date; no final residual drift remains. |
| 8 | VES 5,000 at 50 | USD 25, then USD 75 at 50 | VES and USD residuals decrease after each payment and finish at zero. |
| 9 | VES 5,000 at 50 | VES 2,000, then VES 3,000 | Local residual changes from 5,000 to 3,000 to 0. |
| 10 | VES 5,000 at 50 | USD 25 at 50, VES 1,050 at 52, USD 50 at 54 | Mixed-date reconciliation settles the invoice and exposes the rate impact without corrupting helper fields. |

## Assertions Per Matrix Scenario

Each scenario must assert:

1. The invoice posts in the requested document currency.
2. `fcurrency_id` is USD.
3. Document, VES, and USD totals are correct at the invoice date.
4. Native and dual residuals are correct after every payment.
5. `account.partial.reconcile.amount_fcurrency` uses the reference-currency
   partial when either reconciled side is in USD, and otherwise converts at the
   reconciliation date.
6. Reference and local payment widgets contain the expected non-exchange
   payments in the selected display currency.
7. Journal-item `balance_fcurrency`, `debit_fcurrency`,
   `credit_fcurrency`, and `amount_residual_fcurrency` remain coherent.
8. Intermediate states are `partial` where applicable.
9. The final residual is zero and the final payment state is the valid Odoo
   settled state (`paid` or `in_payment`, depending on installed accounting
   features).
10. Exchange-difference entries generated by Odoo do not create duplicate
    business payments in the assertions.

## Mandatory Reporting Tests

1. Aged receivable defaults to VES and exposes both VES and USD options.
2. Aged receivable in USD honors the report cutoff: a payment after the cutoff
   must not reduce an earlier report.
3. Aged receivable USD partner totals equal the grand total for an isolated
   partner and equal the sum of all period buckets.
4. Aged payable in USD uses the correct sign, partner total, and period bucket.
5. Monetary columns use the currency selected in `dual_currency_id`.

## Mandatory Additional Flows

1. A VES credit note created from a VES invoice reuses the original invoice
   rate for reference fields.
2. A USD credit note uses its own accounting date for VES conversion.
3. A manual USD journal entry keeps the converted VES debit and credit after
   saving and balances in USD reference fields.
4. Payment helper fields calculate local and reference amounts for both VES and
   USD payments.
5. If the company has no reference currency, computations fall back safely to
   the company currency.
6. `base.VES` is active and Venezuela points to VES after module installation.
7. The manifest has no dependency on `account_report_multi_currency`.

## Mandatory Demo Validation

When demo data is enabled:

1. `l10n_ve_dual_currency.demo_company_ves` exists.
2. Its country is Venezuela, company currency is VES, and reference currency is
   USD.
3. `l10n_ve_dual_currency.demo_rate_usd_20260110` belongs to that company and
   represents 1 USD = 50 VES.

The demo test must skip explicitly when the database was initialized without
demo data. It must execute, not skip, in the dedicated demo-data run.

## Acceptance Rule

A migration is not complete until the module installs and all applicable tests
pass in both runs defined by `TEST_PLAN.md`. Static test files without executed
results do not satisfy this contract.
